Text message terms

Effective October 20, 2026. These terms apply to the Skip Jackson Insurance Services texts program.

Reply STOP to cancel at any time. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Program name

Skip Jackson Insurance Services texts.

How you join

You join by checking the optional box on a form on this website, or by giving written consent, for example by replying YES to a confirmation text I send after you ask. Messages come from [phone number used for texting].

When you agree, you're allowing me to call and text you at the number you gave about Medicare plans, including calls and texts made with an autodialer or an artificial or prerecorded voice. Agreeing to receive texts is not a condition of buying any plan, product, or service.

What messages you'll receive

  • Appointment reminders
  • Responses to your questions
  • Plan information you've asked for

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

How to stop

Reply STOP, QUIT, END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, REVOKE, or OPT OUT, or tell me in any other reasonable way, and I'll stop within 10 business days at most. You'll get one message confirming that you've been unsubscribed, and then no more texts unless you sign up again. You can also call or email me to stop.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message, call (404) 354-7597, or email Sjackson@empowerbrokerage.com.

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Please don't text your Medicare number, Social Security number, or health details. See the privacy policy for more.
